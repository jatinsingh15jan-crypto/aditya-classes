Shiv Kumar

Founder & Lead Teacher, Aditya Classes

With an M.Tech background, Shiv Kumar brings strong technical knowledge and a logical way of thinking into every lesson. He is calm, approachable and patient, and students feel comfortable asking any doubt, however small. He believes that marks follow understanding, so he explains the "why" behind every formula and step instead of pushing students to memorise.

His teaching style is simple and step by step. He connects difficult topics in Maths and Physics to everyday examples, solves problems on the board together with students, and keeps revisiting basics until they are clear. Regular tests, honest feedback and personal attention help each student improve at their own pace and build real confidence for the board exams.
